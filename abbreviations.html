A
AfCFTA
African Continental Free Trade Area
Trade agreement progressively opening markets across 55 African Union member states. Relevant to the sell-side marketplace for WSSF products.
ECO
AfDB
African Development Bank
Multilateral development finance institution. Potential source of concessional financing for the RWN SPV.
ECO
AU
African Union
Continental body whose Agenda 2063 frames inclusive development goals. Referenced in the RWN Strategic Plan alignment section.
SP
B
BDF
Business Development Fund
Rwandan government fund supporting SME development. Relevant as a potential partner or co-financier for WSSF businesses in the finance-side marketplace.
ECO
BNR
Banque Nationale du Rwanda (National Bank of Rwanda)
Rwanda's central bank and financial regulator. The SPV must engage BNR for non-deposit lending institution licensing.
ECO
BP
Business Plan
Required document for revolving fund loan eligibility. A structured template is provided in the fund strategy annexes.
RF
C
CNFS
Climate and Nature Finance Strategy (2024–2030)
Rwanda's national strategy for mobilising climate and nature finance. RWN's Environmental Justice Program aligns with this; relevant for green IGA financing on the finance-side marketplace.
SP
CSO
Civil Society Organisation
Non-governmental, non-profit organisations. RWN operates as a CSO and leads the Rwanda CSO SDG5 Forum.
SP
CSR
Corporate Social Responsibility
Corporate programmes that may provide sponsorship or pre-purchase agreements for WSSF products on the finance-side marketplace.
ECO
D
DFI
Development Finance Institution
Institutions like IFC, AfDB, Proparco that provide concessional loans and equity to development-oriented vehicles. The SPV is designed to attract DFI capital.
ECO
E
EJP
Environmental Justice Program
RWN's programme addressing environmental degradation, climate change, and GBV. Strategic objective SO7. Closely linked to green IGA opportunities in the revolving fund.
SP
F
FP
Family Planning
Access to contraception and reproductive health services. Mentioned in the SP as a core health service gap for rural Rwandan women.
SP
G
GBV
Gender-Based Violence
Violence directed at individuals based on gender. A core focus area of RWN across all programmes. Economic vulnerability linked to GBV is a key rationale for the revolving fund.
SP
GGCRS
Green Growth and Climate Resilience Strategy
Rwanda's national strategy for mainstreaming climate resilience. RWN's EJP aligns with this strategy.
SP
I
IFC
International Finance Corporation
World Bank Group's private sector arm. Active in East African microfinance and women's economic empowerment financing. Potential DFI partner for RWN's SPV.
ECO
IGA
Income-Generating Activity
Any business or economic activity that generates income. WSSFs must have a functioning IGA to be eligible for revolving fund loans.
RF
INGO
International Non-Governmental Organisation
International civil society organisations. RWN strategy mentions building consortia with INGOs to access back-donor funding.
SP
M
M&E
Monitoring and Evaluation
Systems for tracking programme performance and outcomes. The M&E Officer sits on the revolving fund management body.
RF
MEAL
Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning
RWN's integrated performance management framework. Identified as a weakness in the SWOT: "Weak MEAL system at organization level."
SP
MFI
Microfinance Institution
Financial institutions providing small loans to low-income clients. Commercial MFIs in Rwanda charge 3–5%+ per month — higher than RWN's proposed 2%. The graduation pathway should link WSSFs to MFIs after multiple fund cycles.
ECO
MoMo
Mobile Money (MTN / Airtel)
Mobile-based payment and transfer system widely used in Rwanda. The digital fund management platform integrates with MoMo APIs for repayment tracking.
ECO
N
NCD
Non-Communicable Disease
Chronic diseases such as breast and cervical cancer. Emerging health challenge for Rwandan women identified in the SP.
SP
NDC
Nationally Determined Contribution
Rwanda's climate commitments under the Paris Agreement. RWN's EJP aligns with NDC targets on renewable energy and sustainable agriculture.
SP
NGO
Non-Governmental Organisation
RWN's legal category. The SP notes the enabling legal environment for RWN to operate both as a CSO and a social enterprise.
SP
NST1
National Strategy for Transformation 1 (2017–2024)
Rwanda's first national transformation strategy, covering economic, social, and governance transformation. RWN's SP 2020–2030 aligns its pillars to NST1 and NST2.
SP
NST2
National Strategy for Transformation 2 (2024–2029)
Rwanda's current national transformation strategy. Targets creation of 1.25 million productive jobs. RWN's revolving fund and marketplace directly support this goal.
SP
O
ODA
Official Development Assistance
Government-to-government or government-to-multilateral aid flows. Current global contraction (USAID freeze) is a key threat to RWN's donor-dependent funding model.
ECO
R
RDB
Rwanda Development Board
Government agency supporting investment and business development in Rwanda. Potential partner for SME support programmes linked to the revolving fund.
ECO
RF
Revolving Fund Strategy (document)
Source document tag used throughout this platform: "RWN Revolving Fund Strategy for Economic Empowerment of Women Safe Space Facilitators" (August 2025).
RF
RWF
Rwandan Franc
Rwanda's national currency. All loan amounts in the fund are denominated in RWF. Min: RWF 300,000 / Max: RWF 916,000.
RF
RWN
Rwanda Women's Network
Non-governmental organisation established in 1995, dedicated to promoting and strengthening strategies that empower Rwandan women. Fund owner and manager.
SP
S
SACCO
Savings and Credit Cooperative Organisation
Member-owned financial cooperative. Umurenge SACCOs are Rwanda's government-backed grassroots financial institutions. The revolving fund must differentiate itself from and complement (not compete with) SACCOs.
ECO
SDG
Sustainable Development Goal
UN global goals for 2030. RWN's SP aligns with SDG 3 (Good Health), SDG 5 (Gender Equality), SDG 8 (Decent Work), SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities), SDG 13 (Climate Action).
SP
SO
Strategic Objective
Numbered objectives in RWN's 2020–2030 Strategic Plan. SO1 (health/GBV), SO2 (GBV protection), SO3 (socio-economic empowerment), SO4 (knowledge/skills), SO5 (governance/leadership), SO6 (institutional development), SO7 (environmental justice).
SP
SP
Strategic Plan (document)
Source document tag used throughout this platform: "Rwanda Women's Network 2020–2030 Strategic Plan" (December 2022).
SP
SPV
Special Purpose Vehicle
A legally separate entity created by RWN to own and manage the revolving fund capital. Ring-fences fund liabilities from RWN operations and enables institutional investment. Proposed as non-deposit lending institution regulated by BNR.
ECO
SRHR
Sexual and Reproductive Health Rights
Rights related to sexual health, reproductive choices, and bodily autonomy. A core service area in RWN's health programme.
SP
SWOT
Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
Strategic analysis framework. Used in the SP and in our revolving fund analysis (see "What Is Missing" page).
SP
V
VSLA
Village Savings and Loan Association
Community-based savings groups. Mentioned in the SP M&E framework (Output 2: women in VSLAs). Complementary to but distinct from the revolving fund — VSLAs use members' own savings; the fund uses external capital.
SP
W
WASH
Water, Sanitation and Hygiene
A cross-cutting health and development domain. Mentioned in the SP's NST2 alignment section.
SP
WSS
Women Safe Space
Community hubs established by RWN providing integrated services: health, GBV support, governance, and socio-economic empowerment. 24 WSS active across 12 districts. The revolving fund is designed for WSS Facilitators.
RF
WSSF
Women Safe Space Facilitator
Frontline volunteers who run the WSS model at community level. Primary and exclusive target group of the revolving fund. Must have a business plan, bank account, RWN agreement, and active IGA to be eligible.
