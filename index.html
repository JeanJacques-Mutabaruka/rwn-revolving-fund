Strategic Context

How the Revolving Fund fits in RWN's Strategy

The revolving fund is explicitly positioned under Strategic Objective SO3 of the 2020–2030 Strategic Plan. It feeds Strategic Result 2: Women Socio-Economically Empowered. The three pillars of RWN's theory of change frame the fund's purpose.

Pillar 01
💚
Heal
Safe Spaces provide the trust infrastructure on which the fund is built. WSSFs are the very facilitators of healing — the fund reinforces their stability and self-sufficiency.
Pillar 02
💪
Empower
The revolving fund is RWN's core economic empowerment vehicle: access to capital + capacity building + market linkage for frontline women leaders.
Pillar 03
🌱
Transform
Economically empowered WSSFs become stronger community leaders, driving governance, GBV prevention, and environmental resilience in their districts.
