Women Safe Space Facilitators (WSSFs) — frontline volunteers who run the WSS model at community level, described as "agents of protection and advocacy" and "role models and changemakers." RF

  • Have a bank account (personal or group)

Interest accrual — as stated RF
Interest accrues from the date of disbursement. Principal repayment begins after the grace period. Maximum repayment: 12 months.

Collateral RF

Group guarantee
Signed commitment in front of local authorities — replaces traditional collateral requirements, making the fund accessible for low-income women.
Business proposal
A clear and feasible business plan is required as part of the collateral package. Encourages trust and collective responsibility.
4 · Loan Cycle

7-step documented process RF

1
Application
Submission of loan request + simple business plan. Can be by template, hard copy, email, online signed copy, or in-person/phone presentation.
2
Appraisal
Verification by RWN Field Staff in the targeted area — within 1 week.
3
Approval
Loan Committee decision — within 1 week. Two-level approval: Field Staff (Level 1) then Program & Finance Team (Level 2).
4
Disbursement
Transfer to beneficiary's bank account.
5
Follow-up
Monthly monitoring visits and business coaching by field staff.
6
Repayment
Scheduled repayments tracked with receipts or payslips.
7
Re-loaning
Eligible after full repayment and positive impact review. Requires new business plan + achievement of milestones + group business insurance.
5 · Governance Structure

Management and oversight RF

Management Body

Multi-disciplinary team composed of: Program Manager, M&E Officer, Project Coordinator Representative, Field Staff Representative.

Oversight Body

Internal Audit Team: Finance Manager + 2 Finance Officers. External Evaluator if needed (quarterly). Upholds accountability and transparency, builds donor and community confidence.
6 · Capacity Building

Pre- and post-loan support RF

📚
Pre-loan Training
Refresher training on: financial literacy, record keeping, savings culture, and business planning. Required before loan disbursement.
🤝
Post-loan Mentorship
Quarterly peer-learning and group coaching sessions. Ongoing mentorship from field staff through monthly monitoring visits.
🔗
Market Linkage
Access to market linkage and skills improvement via partnerships with RWN stakeholders. (Mechanism not yet specified.)
🛡️
Loan Insurance
Optional group insurance for health and emergencies affecting business continuity. Group business insurance required at re-loaning stage.
7 · Operational Tools

Templates provided in annexes RF

📋 Business Plan Template
7 sections: Personal/Group Info, Business Description, Financial Details, Loan Utilization, Repayment Plan, Social Impact, Endorsement (incl. local authority signature).
📝 Loan Application Form
Fields: applicant name, Safe Space affiliation, business type, loan amount, purpose, repayment schedule, signature, date. For digital or print submission.
📅 Repayment Schedule
12-month tracker with: payment due, amount paid, balance, receipt number, signature. Paper-based.
🗂️ Monitoring Checklist
For field staff: date, facilitator visited, business observed, progress since last visit, challenges, support provided, next steps, field staff signature.
📊 Financial Projections Table
Structure exists with correct columns: capital disbursed, interest income, recovery rate, gross benefit, operational costs, net benefit. Rows are empty — no figures.
📖 Policy Manual
Referenced as governing document covering eligibility, loan cycle, interest, defaults, disputes, re-loaning. Not included in the shared document.
8 · Reporting & Accountability

Accountability framework as stated RF

📆 Quarterly Reports
Submitted to RWN management and donors. Covers repayments, usage, and outcomes — done by Field Officers.
🔍 Annual Audits
To assess financial health and evaluate social impact of the fund.
📖 Success Stories
Documented to inform advocacy, visibility, and potential replication or scale-up to other WSS or NGOs.
